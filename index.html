About Me

Hey! I'm Isna — a creative writer & storyteller crafting engaging digital experiences.

My work focus on bringing structure to raw ideas and transforming complex concepts into clear, beautiful stories. I care deeply about narrative flow, reader engagement, and creating written experiences that truly resonate with people. With a background in Sociology and sales & marketing, I bring a unique analytical perspective to narrative crafting. My writing journey spans published works on international and national non-profit platforms, including Hivos (Netherlands) and IIED, as well as feature pieces for Tanoker Magazine.

Beyond digital articles and research papers, I have co-authored two books in 2020—covering inspiring real-life stories and facilitator training modules. Recently, I’ve been crafting strategic promotional copy for e-commerce groups while working as a freelance writer for web articles, social media captions, and academic papers.

I love bringing structure to raw ideas and creating clear, engaging content that truly resonates with readers.
